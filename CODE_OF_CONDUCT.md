# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text is at that link and is incorporated here by reference.

## In short

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Expected: respectful, constructive feedback; owning mistakes; focusing on what is best for the project.

Not acceptable: harassment, trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([@markvrma](https://github.com/markvrma)) is responsible for enforcement and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors.

To report a problem, open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/markvrma/brigd/issues) titled "Conduct" (keep details minimal in public) or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled with discretion. Enforcement follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1 enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
